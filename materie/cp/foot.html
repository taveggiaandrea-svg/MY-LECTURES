Study rework of the course material of 30509 Computer Programming, Bocconi University, academic year 2025/2026 (course director L. Saglietti, teaching assistant D. Straziota). Lesson 1 follows the lecture of 8 September 2025: Introduction to the course and Introduction to the Python scientific stack: numpy, scipy & matplotlib, parts 1 to 3. Lesson 2 follows the lecture of 11 September 2025: the handwritten notes Storing arrays in memory, the slides on ndarray internals and constructors, and the script 0_rand_sum_demo.py. Lesson 3 follows the lecture of 15 September 2025: the script 01_pi_estimate.py and its tasks, the solution 02_mc_pi_est_proj.py, and the slides on indexing, views and broadcasting.

Quizzes also draw on the course exercise sheet Numpy and pyplot exercises (with its official solutions) and on the course list of theory questions. Further material named in the lecture plan: the numpy quickstart and user guide, the scipy tutorial and the matplotlib pyplot tutorial.

Definitions, examples and numbers are those of the slides, notes and scripts; text and figures are rewritten. Timings measured on another machine, simulated Monte Carlo points and examples marked as added on this page are not in the sources. Personal study use only: do not share.
