Study rework of the course material of 30416 Big Data and Databases, Bocconi University, academic year 2026/2027 (course director L. Molteni). Slides by A. Rezzani: lesson 1, The data analytics timeline: from mainframes to predictive analytics; lesson 2, Querying a relational database: the SQL language part I; lesson 3, Querying a relational database: the SQL language part II; lesson 4, The big data toolbox: NoSQL databases, distributed computation and Hadoop; lesson 5, Hadoop: the big data "Ecosystem"; lessons 6 and 7, Lightning fast computing with Spark; lesson 8, Big Data Architectures. Integrating the big data tools in the company's information system. Slides by L. Molteni: lesson 9, Predictive vs Generative AI, Data Structures, Business Use Cases.

Datasets come from the course SQL scripts: lesson 2 (SQL Basics, PK and FK), lesson 3 (07.union.sql, 08.joins.sql and the OrdersDB scripts) and from the tables shown in the slides; Spark scripts of lesson 6 (sales.csv, word count, RDD, DataFrame and SQL scripts) and lesson 7 (structured streaming, GraphFrames, logistic regression and linear regression notebooks). The predictions of the lesson 7 logistic regression were obtained by running the course script with Spark 3.5.
