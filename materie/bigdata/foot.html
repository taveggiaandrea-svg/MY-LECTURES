Study rework of the course material of 30416 Big Data and Databases, Bocconi University, academic year 2026/2027 (course director L. Molteni). Slides by A. Rezzani: lesson 1, The data analytics timeline: from mainframes to predictive analytics; lesson 2, Querying a relational database: the SQL language part I; lesson 3, Querying a relational database: the SQL language part II; lesson 4, The big data toolbox: NoSQL databases, distributed computation and Hadoop.

Datasets come from the course SQL scripts: lesson 2 (SQL Basics, PK and FK), lesson 3 (07.union.sql, 08.joins.sql and the OrdersDB scripts) and from the tables shown in the slides. Further readings suggested in lesson 1: R. Kimball and M. Ross, The Data Warehouse Toolkit, 3rd edition; Fundamentals of Business Intelligence, Springer. Course textbooks: P. Wilton and J. W. Colby, Beginning SQL, Wrox, 2005; N. Dasgupta, Practical Big Data Analytics, Packt, 2018.

Definitions, examples and numbers are those of the slides and scripts; text and figures are rewritten. Examples marked as illustrative, simulated or added on this page are not in the slides. Personal study use only: do not share.
